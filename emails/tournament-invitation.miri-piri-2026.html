14th Annual Miri Piri Canada Soccer Tournament 2026

Organizer: BC Tigers FC

📅  July 3, 4 & 5, 2026 📍  Newton Athletic Park, 7395 128 Street, Surrey, BC V3W 2M7

Dear Club Presidents, League Organizers, Coaches & Team Managers,

BC Tigers FC warmly invites your club and teams to the 14th Annual Miri Piri Soccer Tournament (Miri Piri Canada Soccer Cup) at Newton Athletic Park, Surrey, BC. Teams from across Canada are coming together for thrilling matches, top-level competition, and a high-energy community soccer atmosphere. The tournament features youth (U6 to U19), adult, and masters divisions with a $70,000 total prize pool. All prize money is sponsored.

Enjoy refreshments while you watch the games, free appetizers/lunch on Saturday and Sunday, and free parking for all teams and families. Tournament kickoff is Friday evening, July 3. Registration is first-come, first-served while spots remain.
